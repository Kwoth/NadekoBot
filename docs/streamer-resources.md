## Discord Community Building

Your Discord server is your community hub. Tips for growth:

1. **Use stream notifications** - Keep your community informed when you go live
2. **Create engagement channels** - Clips, highlights, and discussion
3. **Reward active members** - Use NadekoBot's XP and leveling system
4. **Cross-promote** - Link your Discord in your stream panels
