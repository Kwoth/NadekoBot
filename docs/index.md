# NadekoBot Documentation

Welcome to the official NadekoBot documentation. NadekoBot is a powerful, open-source Discord bot built for content creators and streaming communities.

## Why NadekoBot?

NadekoBot is trusted by thousands of Discord servers to:

- **Notify your community** when you go live on Twitch or Kick
- **Moderate chat** with customizable filters and commands
- **Engage viewers** with games, leveling, and rewards
- **Track stream stats** and community growth
